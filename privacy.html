Pop:Up for macOS

Privacy Policy

Your videos are processed on your Mac. Here is what Pop:Up stores and how this support website works.

Last updated: 8 November 2026

Who provides Pop:Up

Pop:Up is published by Nelia Perskaia. For support, privacy questions or requests concerning support correspondence, contact getpopup@proton.me.

This policy explains the macOS application, this support website and information you choose to send by email.

Your media stays under your control

Pop:Up opens and processes the videos, photos and folders you choose on your Mac. The app does not upload your media or drawings to a developer-operated server.

Pop:Up does not require a separate Pop:Up account. An Apple Account is used for App Store subscriptions. The app contains no advertising, tracking or third-party analytics SDK. There is no developer-operated cloud library.

Subscriptions and Apple payments

Subscriptions, introductory offers and payments are managed by Apple through the App Store. Pop:Up uses StoreKit to read product prices and verify subscription transactions and access on your Mac. We do not receive your card number or billing details through the app, and the app does not transmit purchase history or transaction identifiers to a developer-operated server. Apple processes purchases under its own terms and privacy policy. You can manage or cancel your subscription in your Apple Account settings.

Information stored on your Mac

Pop:Up stores interface preferences, recent and pinned folder locations, permission bookmarks and a local color-tag index in its macOS app storage. These settings help the app restore your workspace and folder access.

Media thumbnails and analysis drawings are held in memory during use. This release does not save analysis projects or export annotated video.

Audio waveforms are calculated locally and stored as small peak envelopes in the app cache, with up to 64 recent envelopes. This cache does not contain a copy of the original video or audio.

Changes to your files

Playback and drawing do not rewrite video or photo content. When you edit Finder tags or color labels, Pop:Up writes tag metadata to the selected original file.

These tags are visible to Finder and other apps. They remain on the file until you remove them.

Permissions, retention and deletion

You choose which files and folders Pop:Up can access through macOS. Saved settings and folder references remain locally until removed or reset. Removing the app alone may leave its local settings behind.

Unpinning a folder removes that pinned entry; it does not delete the folder, its media, its Finder tags or any separate recent-folder entry. You can remove Finder tags using Pop:Up or Finder.

Removing Pop:Up’s local app data does not remove your original media or its Finder tags. Drawings and in-memory thumbnails are not kept as a separate cloud copy.

Waveform envelopes can be recreated from the originals. They are removed as older cache entries are replaced or when the app cache is cleared; clearing this cache does not delete your media.

Diagnostics and Apple services

Pop:Up may write technical playback timings and file-operation errors to local macOS diagnostics. It does not send its own telemetry to the publisher.

macOS, iCloud Drive, the Mac App Store and any email or web browser you use operate under their own privacy settings and policies.

When you contact support

If you email us, we receive your email address, message and any attachments you choose to send. We use this information to respond to your request and investigate the issue.

Support correspondence is kept as needed to handle your request and related follow-up, and to meet applicable legal obligations. You may contact us to request deletion of support correspondence. Information that must be retained to meet a legal obligation may be kept for that purpose.

Send only what is needed to explain the issue, and only media you have permission to share. Support email is handled separately from the app’s local video processing. Email providers process messages under their own terms and privacy policies.

This support website

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages. We do not add analytics scripts, advertising pixels, contact forms or browser storage to these pages.

GitHub Pages logs and stores visitors’ IP addresses for security purposes, whether or not they are signed in to GitHub. See GitHub Pages data collection and the GitHub Privacy Statement.

Clicking the support email link opens your email application; it does not submit a message through this website.

Changes and questions

We will update this policy if Pop:Up’s data practices change. The date at the top identifies the latest revision.

Privacy enquiries: getpopup@proton.me.
